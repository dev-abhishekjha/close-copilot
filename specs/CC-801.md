# CC-801 · Document corpus

## Goal

The documents the agent will search: one accounting policy manual, one contract per supplier for both companies, and close notes for the history months, all as Markdown sources with committed PDFs.

## Context

- Ticket: `docs/implementation-tickets.md`, section `CC-801`. Plan row: Phase 3, domain-data-engineer, standard; graph check "Thresholds in the policy equal rules.yaml (test)".
- There is enough material that retrieval genuinely matters: dozens of contracts across two companies plus notes that change month to month. Contracts and notes are **generated from the profiles** so they agree with the seeded world. Render everything to PDF so ingestion (CC-803, via docling) exercises real document parsing. The Markdown stays as the source.
- **Policy** `corpus/shared/POL-001-accounting-policy.md`, 10–15 pages, numbered sections. These section numbers are fixed because the golden citations (CC-907) point at them:
  - §1 Scope, §2 Close calendar
  - §3 Fixed assets (§3.1 capitalisation threshold ₹50,000)
  - §4 Prepaid expenses (§4.2 spread annual costs monthly)
  - §5 Accruals (§5.1 accrue recurring suppliers at their usual amount)
  - §6 GST and input tax credit (§6.1 claim credit only for invoices in GSTR-2B; §6.2 follow up with suppliers)
  - §7 Bank reconciliation (§7.1 book bank charges monthly; §7.2 book gateway settlements with fee and GST)
  - §8 Cut-off (§8.1 expenses belong to their service period)
  - §9 Journal approvals (maker-checker, limits)
- Planned citations (plan, RAG evaluation design): prepaid → POL-001 §4.2; capitalisation → §3.1; GSTR-2B → §6.1 or §6.2; bank charge → §7.1; gateway → §7.2; accrual → §5.1; cut-off → §8.1; duplicate → the supplier's contract. Each of those sections must be a heading the chunker can split on and must state its rule in one clear paragraph.
- **Contract** `corpus/<company>/contracts/CON-<supplier-id>.md`, one per profile supplier (17 each in `sharma.yaml` and `mehta.yaml`, so 34 in all; acceptance needs at least 30): parties, service, billing frequency (monthly or annual upfront), amount, GST, service period, payment terms.
- **Close note** `corpus/<company>/notes/NOTE-<company>-<YYYY-MM>.md` for each history month: April, May and June 2026 for both companies, so 6 notes. Example line: "Sahyadri Power's bill usually arrives around the 8th of the next month; accrue at the usual amount."
- Quoted from `config/rules.yaml` (the values the policy must equal; amounts are whole rupees, tolerance is in paise):

```yaml
capitalisation: {threshold_inr: 50000, asset_keywords: [laptop, printer, server, furniture, air conditioner], watched_expense_accounts: ["Repairs and Maintenance"]}
prepaid:        {min_amount_inr: 50000, keywords: [annual, yearly, "12 months", renewal]}
variance:       {pct_threshold: 25, abs_threshold_inr: 50000}
bank_match:     {date_window_days: 3}
gstr2b:         {amount_tolerance_paise: 100}
accruals:       {lookback_months: 4, min_occurrences: 3, amount_band_pct: 20}
```

- Quoted from `config/companies/<id>.yaml` (supplier entries; `Supplier` struct at `internal/company/profile.go:80`): `id, name, kind (rent|utility|subscription|goods|services), billing (empty = monthly | annual), monthly_inr (value or [min,max]), amount_inr, renew_month, day, gst_rate, state_code, registered (default true), start_month, end_month, individual`. An unregistered supplier has no GSTIN and no GST. Example: `{id: codeforge-licences, name: Codeforge Licences, kind: subscription, billing: annual, amount_inr: 360000, renew_month: 4, gst_rate: 18, state_code: "07"}`.
- History months are April to June 2026 (`history_months` in the suite files); the evaluated months are July to September. The corpus is built from profiles and rules only.

- `internal/company/profile.go:50` `type Profile struct` and `:80` `type Supplier struct`; `:162` `type Rules struct` (fields `Capitalisation.ThresholdINR`, `Prepaid.MinAmountINR`, `Variance.PctThreshold`, and so on); `:389` `LoadProfile`, `:408` `LoadProfiles(dir)`, `:460` `LoadRules(path)`. `internal/seed/profile.go:73-80` forwards them as `seed.LoadProfiles` and `seed.LoadRules`; use the `seed` names.

## Subtasks

- [ ] `corpus/shared/POL-001-accounting-policy.md`: 10–15 pages, the numbered sections above, thresholds written as the rule values (₹50,000, 25%, ±3 days, ₹1, 4 months, 3 occurrences, 20%). Each quoted threshold carries a machine-readable marker (an HTML comment such as `<!-- rule:capitalisation.threshold_inr=50000 -->`) so the test can read it without parsing prose.
- [ ] `internal/seed/corpus.go`: `GenerateCorpus` (profiles, rules, output directory) writing `CON-*.md` and `NOTE-*.md` from templates; deterministic (no clock, no map iteration order, no randomness beyond the profile seed). Payment terms come from a stable hash of seed plus supplier id. Money stays integer rupees, no floats (the `nofloat` analyzer covers this package).
- [ ] `corpus/gen.go` (`//go:build ignore`): `go run corpus/gen.go` regenerates the contracts, notes and every PDF from the Markdown; `-check` writes to a temp directory and fails if the committed files differ.
- [ ] PDF rendering: pandoc is **not** installed on the owner's machine and needs a TeX engine, so use a pure-Go renderer (recommended `codeberg.org/go-pdf/fpdf`; any maintained pure-Go library is acceptable) with a fixed creation date and no compression, so output is byte-stable. Real text, headings and tables, not images, so docling can parse them. Commit the PDFs next to their Markdown.
- [ ] Tests in `internal/seed/corpus_test.go` (names start with `TestCorpus`): (1) policy markers equal `rules.yaml` values, and the test fails when a rule is changed in memory; (2) every policy section in the list above exists as a heading; (3) one contract per profile supplier and each contract's billing frequency, amount or range, GST rate and service period equal the profile; (4) at least 6 notes, one per company and history month; (5) every `.md` has a matching `.pdf` that starts with `%PDF` and is larger than 1 KB; (6) regenerating gives the committed bytes.

## Acceptance (human-readable)

- The corpus holds one policy, at least 30 contracts and at least 6 close notes, all as PDFs.
- Contract terms match the profiles (test); policy numbers match the rules (test).
- A person opens the policy PDF and the sections read as a policy manual, with no placeholder text.
- No contract or note mentions a planted error, an eval month (2026-07 to 2026-09) or ground truth.

## Out of scope

- Ingestion, chunking and embedding (CC-803), the docling client (CC-802), hybrid search (CC-804).
- Golden citations and retrieval queries (`evals/golden/**`, `evals/dev/**`; CC-804 and CC-907).
- Contracts or notes for any company beyond `sharma` and `mehta`.
- Editing `config/rules.yaml` or the profiles. If a rule and the policy disagree, the policy follows the rule.
