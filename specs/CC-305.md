# CC-305 · Evidence writers: bank CSV and GSTR-2B

## Goal

Derive each month's `bank.csv` and `gstr2b.json` from the true world, never from the books.

## Context

- Ticket: `docs/implementation-tickets.md`, section `CC-305`. The file formats are the shared specs "Bank statement: `data/external/<company>/<YYYY-MM>/bank.csv`" and "GSTR-2B: `data/external/<company>/<YYYY-MM>/gstr2b.json`". Read both; they are the contract the loaders (CC-402, CC-403) parse.
- **Input is the world (CC-302).** Read `internal/seed/world.go`:
  - `World{Company, Month, OpeningBank, Events, ClosingBank}`;
  - `Event.BankDelta()` is non-zero exactly for the cash events: receipts, gateway settlements and interest in; vendor payments, payroll and bank charges out;
  - gateway receipts go to the clearing account and never hit the bank;
  - `BankRef`, `Party`, `InvoiceNo`, `Meta.SupplierID`, `Meta.SupplierGSTIN`, `Meta.GSTRate` and `Meta.Fee`/`FeeGST` on settlements.
- **The bank sees what the books don't.** The planter (CC-306) later removes events from the *books'* view, e.g. a bank charge. The writers here take a `World` and write what it holds; they never read ERPNext.
- **No floats** (`nofloat` covers `internal/seed`). Rupee amounts are `money.Paise.Rupees()` strings. In JSON, emit them as numbers with exactly two decimals using `json.Number`, never `float64`.
- `data/` is git-ignored. Tests and acceptance write under `tmp/` or `t.TempDir()`.
- No new dependencies.

## Subtasks (Phase 1: `suite-skeleton`, company `sharma`, one month at a time)

- [ ] **`internal/seed/bankcsv.go`:**
  - `BankLines(w World) ([]BankLine, error)`:
    - one line per event with `BankDelta() != 0`, in the world's event order (already `(Date, ExtID)`);
    - `BankLine{TxnID, Date, ValueDate, Narration, Ref, Withdrawal, Deposit, Balance, ExtID}`;
    - `TxnID = BNK-YYYYMMDD-NNN`, where NNN counts from 001 per date;
    - the running balance starts at `w.OpeningBank`;
    - the last balance must equal `w.ClosingBank`, else an error;
    - `ValueDate` = date.
  - **Narrations**, upper case, deterministic, no randomness:
    - vendor payment: `NEFT-<PARTY>` (or `NEFT-RENT-<MON>-<PARTY>` for rent);
    - receipt: `UPI/<ref>/<PARTY>` or `NEFT-<PARTY>`, chosen from the ref format;
    - payroll: `SALARY BATCH <MON> <YYYY>`;
    - bank charge: `SMS/ACCT CHARGES INCL GST`;
    - gateway settlement: `PG SETTL <MMDD> BATCH <n>`, with n derived from `BankRef`;
    - interest: `INT CREDIT`.

    Truncate party names to fit about 40 characters.
  - `Ref` = `BankRef`, which is empty for charges and interest.
  - `ExtID` is for ground truth (CC-306) and is **not** written to the CSV.
  - `WriteBankCSV(path string, lines []BankLine) error`:
    - writes the header `txn_id,date,value_date,narration,ref,withdrawal,deposit,balance` exactly;
    - two-decimal rupees, exactly one of withdrawal and deposit set and the other empty, `\n` line endings, `encoding/csv` quoting;
    - writes atomically: a temp file, then rename.
- [ ] **`internal/seed/gstr2b.go`:**
  - `GSTR2BOptions{LateShareBP int}`: the share of registered-supplier invoices filed late, in basis points. The default is 500 (5%).
    - Whether an invoice is late depends only on its ExtID: FNV-1a of the ExtID, mod 10000, below `LateShareBP`.
    - So the same invoice is always late or always on time.
  - `BuildGSTR2B(p Profile, period string, worlds []World, opt GSTR2BOptions) (GSTR2B, error)`. Period M holds:
    - the purchases dated in M from registered suppliers that are **not** late;
    - plus the late ones dated in M−1, if M−1's world is among `worlds`.

    Unregistered suppliers never appear, and sales never do.
  - Shape, per the shared spec:
    - `data.gstin` = the company GSTIN; `rtnprd` = `MMYYYY`;
    - `docdata.b2b[]` grouped by `ctin`, each with `trdnm` (the supplier name) and `inv[]` sorted by `(dt, inum)`;
    - `inum` = InvoiceNo, `dt` = `dd-mm-yyyy`, `val` = Gross, `txval` = Taxable, `igst`/`cgst`/`sgst`, `itcavl: "Y"`;
    - `b2b` sorted by `ctin`.
    - Also return, outside the JSON, which ExtIDs were included and which were late, so CC-306 can write "expected" wrong-period items.
  - `WriteGSTR2B(path string, g GSTR2B) error`: 2-space indent, trailing newline, atomic write.
- [ ] **`cmd/seed/main.go`:**
  - `seed evidence --company sharma --month 2026-09 [--small] [--config config/companies] [--out data/external]`;
  - generates the month's world and the previous month's (for late filers);
  - writes `<out>/<company>/<month>/bank.csv` and `gstr2b.json`;
  - needs no ERP variables, like `world`;
  - prints a one-line JSON summary (line count, opening, closing, invoices, late);
  - keep `world`, `bootstrap` and `books` unchanged.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `TestBankCSVBalance`: for several months (2026-04 … 2026-12), `Small` on and off:
    - closing = opening + the sum of cash events;
    - every balance column equals the running sum;
    - exactly one of withdrawal and deposit is set;
    - txn_ids are unique and match `^BNK-\d{8}-\d{3}$`;
    - lines are in date order.
  - `TestBankCSVGolden`: sharma 2026-09 `Small` equals `testdata/evidence/bank-sharma-2026-09-small.csv` (`-update` regenerates it); the header line is exact.
  - `TestGSTR2BCoverage`: over consecutive months 2026-04 … 2026-12, every registered-supplier purchase appears in exactly one period's GSTR-2B, and no unregistered supplier or sale ever does. The last month's late ones are excluded from the check.
  - `TestGSTR2BGolden`: sharma 2026-09 `Small` with 2026-08 equals `testdata/evidence/gstr2b-sharma-2026-09-small.json`.
  - `TestGSTR2BShape`:
    - the JSON decodes into a struct mirroring the shared-spec example;
    - every `ctin` passes `ValidGSTIN` and its first two digits are the supplier's state code;
    - `val = txval + igst + cgst + sgst`;
    - amounts have exactly two decimals;
    - `dt` matches `dd-mm-yyyy`.
  - `TestGSTR2BLateDeterministic`: the same ExtID is always late or always on time; with `LateShareBP` 0 none are late, and with 10000 all are.
  - `cmd/seed/main_test.go`: `evidence` runs with an empty environment into `t.TempDir()` and writes both files.
