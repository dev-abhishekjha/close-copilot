# CC-605 · Missing accrual detector

## Goal

Flag suppliers who bill every month but have no invoice this month, as `missing_accrual` findings keyed on `supplier` and `month`, with the supplier's median amount.

## Context

- Ticket: `docs/implementation-tickets.md`, section `CC-605` (you need not open it; it is quoted below).
- Plan row: Phase 2, implementer, standard risk, depends on CC-601 and CC-204 (both merged); gate-run acceptance `missing_accrual.recall>=6/6`. The graph lists no `phase_2_pass`; the whole ticket is Phase 2, so there is no `suite-skeleton` scope. Phase 1's slice plants only `unrecorded_bank_charge`, so nothing here runs in the skeleton.
- Ticket description: a supplier is "recurring" if it billed in at least `min_occurrences` of the last `lookback_months` months with amounts within `amount_band_pct` of their median. If a recurring supplier has no invoice this month, the expense is probably missing and should be accrued at the median amount.
- Ticket subtasks (quoted): use `list_recurring_suppliers` (books reader) over the months before the one being closed; finding per recurring supplier with no Purchase Invoice and no Journal Entry naming it in the month, amount = median, keys `supplier` and `month`; tests: a new supplier (one month of history) is not flagged, a supplier who skips the month is. Acceptance: all six planted missing accruals are found.
- Shared spec (finding types table): `missing_accrual` | CC-605 | dedupe keys `supplier` + `month`. Ground-truth shape: `{"type": "missing_accrual", "keys": {"supplier": "<ERPNext Supplier name>", "month": "2026-09"}, "amount_paise": <median>}`. `suite-v1` plants 6 (`evals/scenarios/suite-v1.yaml:10`).
- How the planter builds one (CC-306, tickets doc): "Remove a recurring supplier's invoice and payment for the month", changed in books and bank. The planter does not implement `missing_accrual` yet (`internal/seed/plant.go:20` only declares the constant), so recall 6/6 is proven by unit tests on synthetic data only.
- Tool contract: books `list_recurring_suppliers(company, before_month, lookback_months, min_occurrences)` returns `supplier, median_amount, typical_day, months_seen`. The reader here also takes `amountBandPct`. Its window is the `lookback_months` months immediately before `beforeMonth` (it does not include `beforeMonth`).
- Rules (`config/rules.yaml`, `company.AccrualRules`, reached as `in.Rules.Accruals`): `lookback_months: 4`, `min_occurrences: 3`, `amount_band_pct: 20`. Thresholds come only from config; no constants in code. `LoadRules` already validates they are positive and `min_occurrences <= lookback_months`.
- Finding fields (`store.Finding`): `Type, Severity, Title, AmountPaise *money.Paise, Keys map[string]string, Evidence []EvidenceRef, Action *string, Status`. Use `SeverityMedium`, `Action = ActionAccrue` (`"accrue"`), `Status = StatusOpen`. Do not set `Proposal` (the journal proposal is built later by the agent).
- Money is `money.Paise` (`int64` paise). `ledger.RecurringSupplier.MedianAmount` is already paise.
- **No Journal Entry reader exists** on `BooksReader`. "No Journal Entry naming it" must be answered from `GLEntries`: a GL entry in the month with `VoucherType == "Journal Entry"` and `PartyType == "Supplier"` and `Party == supplier` counts as the supplier being accrued or booked. `GLEntry` has `Name, Account, Debit, Credit, PostingDate, VoucherType, VoucherNo, PartyType, Party, IsCancelled, IsOpening, Remarks`. Ignore `IsCancelled` and `IsOpening` rows. Do not add a reader method (that would change `BooksReader`, which fakes in other tests implement).
- "No Purchase Invoice in the month": any `ledger.PurchaseInvoice` with `Supplier == supplier` and `PostingDate` inside the month (readers return submitted documents only and filter by posting date). A return (`IsReturn`) does not count as the month's bill.
- Replay recordings serve only recorded argument sets (`ErrFixtureMissing` otherwise). The check is not wired into the workflow here, so no recordings are needed; do not edit `evals/fixtures/**`.
- Constants: `TypeMissingAccrual` already exists at `internal/checks/finding.go:45`, and so do `ActionAccrue` (`:71`) and the other action and severity names. Add no new exported constant.
