# UI-09 · Proposals and approvals UI

## Goal

A checker can review a pending journal proposal and approve or reject it from clear buttons and confirmation dialogs; a maker sees why they cannot approve their own proposal; and anyone who may see a proposal can open a page that says who proposed, decided and posted it, and when. Every rule and every write stays exactly where it is today.

## Pages

**`/approvals`** (checker only, unchanged route): `PageHeader` "Pending proposals" with the count. Keep `table.approvals`; restyle inside a card wrapper; the columns are exactly the existing ones in the existing order: Proposal (short id, now a link to `/proposals/{id}`), Company, Posting date, Amount (right-aligned, tabular), Lines (nested `table.lines` with account, debit, credit and a totals line, plus a Balanced or Unbalanced notice), Maker, Status (with the status note), Finding (link to `/findings/<id>`), Created (existing format `2026-09-02 10:30 UTC`) and Decision. Decision cell:
  - Controls follow `CanApprove` and `CanReject` (see above). When allowed: a primary **Approve** `OpenDialogButton` and a danger **Reject** `OpenDialogButton`; dialog ids are unique per proposal (`approve-<uuid>` and `reject-<uuid>`).
    - The approve `ConfirmModal` restates what will happen: "Post this journal entry to ERPNext for COMPANY on DATE? Total ₹X, N lines." and posts to `/proposals/{id}/approve` (hidden CSRF).
    - The reject `ConfirmModal` has a required reason `TextareaField` through `Extra` (`name=reason`, `required`, `maxlength=500` through `Attrs`, no Hint or Error on it) and posts to `/proposals/{id}/reject`.
  - **The user's own proposal (`Own`):** render plain `Button`s with `Disabled: true` and `Attrs{"aria-disabled": "true"}` (never an `OpenDialogButton`, so there is no `data-dialog-open` pointing at a missing dialog) with the visible reason "You proposed this; another checker must decide it." and no dialog, form or action URL for that row.
  - **An `approved` row** (posting failed, retry): Approve only, for the recorded checker; others see the status note and no control.
  - After a successful approve, the existing `posted` notice shows as a success card with the journal entry name and its ERPNext link (as today, plus a link to the proposal page). Errors from the server show above the table as alerts with the existing wording.
  - Empty list: `EmptyState` with the existing sentence.

**`GET /proposals/{id}`** (any role, behind `approvalsRoute`, so 501 until Options.Approvals is set and 404 for an unknown or non-UUID id like the other routes): `PageHeader` "Proposal <short id>" with a `StatusChip` (`proposed`, `approved`, `posted`, `rejected`) and a link back to Approvals for checkers or to the finding for others. `Facts`: company, posting date, total, finding (link to `/findings/{id}` when `FindingID` is set), maker, checker (when decided), and the ERPNext journal entry with its link (`rel` and `target` as elsewhere) when posted. The entry: a lines table (account, debit, credit, totals, Balanced notice). A `Timeline`: **Proposed** by the maker at `CreatedAt`; **Approved** or **Rejected** by the checker at `DecidedAt` (for a rejection with its reason, escaped); **Posted** at `PostedAt` with the entry name. Missing times are omitted, not guessed. The decision controls are exactly the list's (built from the same `approvalRow(x, me)` fields: Approve, Reject, the retry-only Approve, the disabled own state, the same dialogs posting to the same routes); the controls are shown **only when the viewer's role is checker** (a gate on the view, not a new rule: `approvalRow` does not look at roles, so the page must not show a maker or viewer the controls or the disabled own state, even though the server would refuse them), and viewers and makers see none. `Get` errors go through `approvalFailed(r, "get", id, err)`: not found is 404, any other error is a logged 500 that shows only "internal error" and never the cause.
