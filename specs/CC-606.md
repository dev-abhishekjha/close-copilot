# CC-606 · Policy rules: capitalisation, prepaid and cut-off

## Goal

Rules from `config/rules.yaml` catch assets booked as expenses, annual costs expensed in one month, and next-month costs booked this month.

Gate-run acceptance from the graph: "4/4 for each of the three types" (the Phase 2 `suite-v1` plants 4 each of `prepaid_not_spread`, `misclassified_expense` and `wrong_period_posting`).
- The rules are deterministic: plain thresholds, keyword lists and account names from `config/rules.yaml`, no model, no hard-coded numbers. Thresholds come only from `Inputs.Rules` (ticket: "thresholds come only from config").
- Ticket description: the checks read invoice line items (account, amount, description). Each finding records the rule that fired (`rules.capitalisation`, `rules.prepaid`, `rules.cutoff`); in E8 the explainer adds the matching policy-manual section.
- Ticket subtasks (quoted):
  - Capitalisation: a line booked to a watched expense account, amount at or above the threshold, and a description containing an asset keyword gives `misclassified_expense`.
  - Prepaid: a line at or above `prepaid.min_amount_inr`, booked to an expense account rather than Prepaid Expenses, with a prepaid keyword in the description, gives `prepaid_not_spread`.
  - Cut-off: parse `Service period: dd-mm-yyyy to dd-mm-yyyy` from descriptions; a period starting after the month ends gives `wrong_period_posting`.
  - Tests per rule, including near-misses (₹49,999; "annual maintenance visit" under the threshold).
- Finding keys (shared spec "Finding types"): `prepaid_not_spread`, `misclassified_expense`, `wrong_period_posting` are all matched on key `invoice` (the Purchase Invoice name, for example `ACC-PINV-2026-00044`). The scorer matches a finding when every key of the ground-truth target is present and equal; extra keys are ignored (`internal/evals/score.go:471`). So `Keys` is exactly `{"invoice": <Purchase Invoice name>}`; put the rule id in the evidence args, not the keys.
- How the planter makes each case (CC-306, `docs/implementation-tickets.md:967-972`; `internal/seed/plant.go` implements only `unrecorded_bank_charge` today): `prepaid_not_spread` = "Book an annual subscription fully to Software Subscriptions"; `misclassified_expense` = "Book the laptop to Repairs and Maintenance"; `wrong_period_posting` = "Post an invoice whose description says 'Service period: 01-10-2026 to 31-10-2026' in September". - Rules, `config/rules.yaml` as shipped (validated by `company.LoadRules`, `internal/company/profile.go:460`): `capitalisation.threshold_inr: 50000`, `capitalisation.asset_keywords: [laptop, printer, server, furniture, air conditioner]`, `capitalisation.watched_expense_accounts: ["Repairs and Maintenance"]`, `prepaid.min_amount_inr: 50000`, `prepaid.keywords: [annual, yearly, "12 months", renewal]`. - Line items: `ledger.PurchaseInvoiceItem{Name, ItemCode, Description, ExpenseAccount string, Amount money.Paise}` (`internal/ledger/models.go:106`). `Amount` is the line's net amount (excludes GST); compare the thresholds against it. `ExpenseAccount` is the ERPNext account name with the company abbreviation suffix, for example `Repairs and Maintenance - STPL` (`company.ERPAccount`, `internal/company/profile.go:153`: `base + " - " + abbr`). - What the seeder's genuine (non-error) bills look like, so the rules do not fire on them (`internal/seed/generate_purchases.go`):

  - The quarterly laptop is booked to **Office Equipment** with `Laptop, 14-inch business notebook for office use (capital asset)` (line 194). It must not fire `misclassified_expense`: Office Equipment is not a watched account.
  - Monthly bills carry `Service period:` for the bill's own month (lines 128-142), so the cut-off rule must not fire on them.
- Month window: read purchase invoices posted in the month. The readers return submitted documents only and filter by `posting_date` (`internal/checks/direct/books.go:112-125`), so the invoices returned for `[first day of M, last day of M]` are the ones "posted in" M. Skip `IsReturn` invoices.
- No GST, supplier or bank data is needed; do not read GL entries or evidence.

## Subtasks

- [ ] `PolicyRulesCheck` (`Name()` returns `"rules"`), `var _ Check = (*PolicyRulesCheck)(nil)`, in `internal/checks/rules.go`. Run: parse `in.Month`; read `PurchaseInvoices(ctx, in.Company, first, last)` for the month (wrap errors with `%w`); skip `IsReturn` invoices; evaluate every item of every invoice against the three rules; return findings sorted by type, then `invoice`.
- [ ] Account matcher (unexported, `rules` prefix): an item's `ExpenseAccount` matches a base name when equal to it or starting with `base + " - "`, case-insensitive, trimmed. Keyword matcher: case-insensitive substring of the item `Description`, keywords trimmed.
- [ ] Capitalisation (`rules.capitalisation`): item account matches one of `Capitalisation.WatchedExpenseAccounts`, `Amount >= Paise(ThresholdINR*100)` (inclusive), and the description contains one of `AssetKeywords` gives `misclassified_expense`. `Action: ActionReclassify`.
- [ ] Prepaid (`rules.prepaid`): `Amount >= Paise(Prepaid.MinAmountINR*100)` (inclusive), the item account does **not** match `Prepaid Expenses`, and the description contains one of `Prepaid.Keywords` gives `prepaid_not_spread`. `Action: ActionBookEntry` (a spreading entry). Define the base name `Prepaid Expenses` as an unexported constant in `rules.go` (it is a chart-of-accounts name the ticket fixes, not a threshold).
- [ ] Cut-off (`rules.cutoff`): find `Service period: dd-mm-yyyy to dd-mm-yyyy` in the item description (case-insensitive, flexible spaces; parse each date with layout `02-01-2006`). If the period's start date is after the last day of the month (that is, on or after the first day of the next month) it gives `wrong_period_posting`; a start on the last day of the month is fine; an unparsable date or a missing period never fires. No amount threshold applies. Skip items whose account matches `Prepaid Expenses` (a next-period cost correctly held in prepaid is not a mis-posting). `Action: ActionBookEntry`.
- [ ] One finding per invoice per type (`Keys{"invoice": inv.Name}`): if several lines of one invoice trigger the same rule, `AmountPaise` is the sum of those lines' `Amount` (a `*money.Paise`, positive) and the title names the invoice. An invoice can legitimately carry two different types (a different `DedupeKey` each).
- [ ] Finding fields: `Severity` medium for all three; `Status: StatusOpen`; `Title` names the invoice, supplier name (`SupplierName`, falling back to `Supplier`), the account, the amount (`money.Paise.Format()`) and the rule, for example `Laptop booked to Repairs and Maintenance (₹62,000.00) on ACC-PINV-2026-00044 from Acme: rules.capitalisation`. Evidence: one `BooksEvidence("list_purchase_invoices", args, inv.Name)` whose args are `{"company": ..., "month": ..., "rule": "rules.capitalisation" | "rules.prepaid" | "rules.cutoff", "account": <the item account of the first triggering line>}` (use a `map[string]string`; for the cut-off rule also add `"service_period_start": "YYYY-MM-DD"`). The `account` arg lets CC-607 link a variance on the same account to the prepaid finding.
- [ ] Table-driven tests in `rules_test.go`, with fake readers, one row per case: each rule fires; amount exactly at the threshold fires and one paisa below does not (₹50,000.00 against ₹49,999.99, and the ₹49,999 near-miss from the ticket); an asset keyword in a non-watched account (Office Equipment laptop) does not fire; a watched account with an asset keyword under the threshold does not fire; "annual maintenance visit" under the prepaid threshold does not fire; a keyword over the threshold in an expense account fires; the seeder's genuine annual bill booked to Prepaid Expenses does not fire (prepaid or cut-off); abbreviation suffix matching (`Repairs and Maintenance - STPL` matches, `Repairs and Maintenance Reserve - STPL` does not); keyword case-insensitivity; a service period starting on the last day of the month does not fire and one starting on the first of the next month does; a malformed or missing service period does not fire; the monthly seeded description with its own month's period does not fire; a custom `Rules` value (threshold 10,000, other keywords) changes the outcome, proving thresholds come from config and not constants; return invoices skipped; two lines on one invoice sum into one finding; one invoice firing two rules gives two findings; findings sorted; evidence carries the rule id and account; reader error wrapped with `%w`; invalid month returns an error; a clean month returns no findings; `TestPolicyRulesFourPlanted`: a month shaped like the planter's output (4 annual subscriptions booked to Software Subscriptions, 4 laptops booked to Repairs and Maintenance, 4 invoices with a next-month "Service period" booked in the month, plus about 10 ordinary and 2 genuine Prepaid Expenses and Office Equipment invoices) gives exactly 12 findings, 4 of each type, with the right `invoice` keys and amounts.
