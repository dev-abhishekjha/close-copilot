# CC-602 · Bank reconciliation matcher

## Goal

Match each bank statement line to the GL entries on the company's bank account, then report unrecorded bank charges, unmatched bank lines, and unmatched ledger entries.

## Context

- Ticket: `docs/implementation-tickets.md`, section `CC-602`.
- Plan row: Phase 1, implementer, standard risk, gates G1–G3.
- Sign convention: a deposit is a debit to the bank account in the books (positive signed amount) and a withdrawal is a credit (negative signed amount). Compare the bank line's signed amount with debit minus credit per voucher.
- Matching passes:
  1. Pass 1: equal amount and equal reference (`reference_no` or `cheque_no` against the bank `ref`).
  2. Pass 2: equal amount and transaction dates within `rules.bank_match.date_window_days` (default 3 days); break ties by closest date, then narration similarity.
- Performance: Index candidate ledger vouchers by amount (`map[money.Paise][]int`) for linear matching across large transaction sets.
- Classification of leftovers:
  - Unmatched bank withdrawal under ₹10,000 (1,000,000 paise) whose narration matches `(?i)CHARGES|CHGS|SMS|FEE|COMMISSION` becomes `unrecorded_bank_charge`.
  - Other unmatched bank lines become `unmatched_bank_line` with a hint (`gateway` when narration starts with `PG SETTL`).
  - Unmatched book vouchers become `unmatched_ledger_entry`.

## Subtasks

- [ ] Group month's GL entries on company's bank accounts by voucher (`VoucherNo`).
- [ ] Implement Pass 1 matching: exact amount and matching reference.
- [ ] Implement Pass 2 matching: exact amount within date window, tie-breaking by date proximity.
- [ ] Classify leftover bank lines and ledger entries into `unrecorded_bank_charge`, `unmatched_bank_line`, and `unmatched_ledger_entry`.
- [ ] Construct `Finding` records with appropriate severity, keys (`bank_txn_id`, `gl_entry`), and `EvidenceRef` items.
- [ ] Implement `Check` interface (`BankRecCheck`).
- [ ] Comprehensive unit tests and benchmarks:
  - Table-driven tests for exact ref matches, date window matches, date tie-breaking.
  - Leftover classification (bank charges vs unmatched vs gateway hint).
  - Clean month matching.
  - Benchmark on 5,000 lines demonstrating linear scaling.

## Acceptance (human-readable)

- Clean control transactions match completely.
- Bank fee lines are flagged as `unrecorded_bank_charge`.
- Gateway and unexplained transactions flagged as `unmatched_bank_line`.
- Unmatched ledger vouchers flagged as `unmatched_ledger_entry`.
- All tests pass under race detector and `make check` passes.
