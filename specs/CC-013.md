# CC-013 · Diagnose the GSTR-2B false alarms on suite-v1

## Goal

A replay of `suite-v1` raises no GSTR-2B finding the ground truth doesn't list, so `precision` can be baselined.

## Context

- Ticket: not in `docs/implementation-tickets.md`. It is a hardening ticket: `tasks/hardening.md`, section `CC-013` (graph entry `CC-013`). Quoted below; don't open the doc.
- Today `make eval-replay SUITE=suite-v1` (results `results/suite-v1/20261012T111528Z`; fixtures `evals/fixtures/suite-v1/` are **untracked in the main checkout only**) gives recall 40/40, precision 59/81 (72.8%), 22 false alarms. 21 of them are GSTR-2B findings with two causes. Diagnose each before changing anything; the fix may be in the seeder, the truth builder or the check.
- **Symptom 1: `gstr2b_wrong_period`, 6 false alarms, all one month off.** The check (`internal/checks/gstr2b.go:213-226`) raises it in the invoice's books month M when the invoice is missing from M's 2B but is in M-1's or M+1's. The truth builder (`internal/seed/truth.go:~238-258`) lists it as an `expected` item in the month whose 2B holds the late invoice, which is M+1 (`GSTR2BOptions.IsLate`: a late invoice dated in M appears in M+1's 2B). Every false alarm has its truth entry one month later: mehta 07 `LTS2026742` (truth in 08), mehta 08 `FBR2026804` (09), sharma 06 `VNT2026631` (07; the one `clean_month` false alarm), sharma 07 `SWF2026735` (08), sharma 08 `SWF2026813` and `SWF2026815` (09). Decide which side is right: file the truth under the books month (M), or change what the check reports. Truth is protected: the owner approves any change to it.
- **Symptom 2: `gstr2b_missing_in_books`, 15 false alarms, all sharma 2026-08.** The fixture's August 2B has 36 entries; the purchase invoices the check read (window 2026-07-01 to 2026-09-30) hold 24 invoices dated August, and 15 of the 2B entries have no matching invoice in that window (for example `BHR/2026/0819`, `OMK/2026/0808`, `VNT/2026/0840`). None is in the truth; only `LDG2026804` is planted. Candidate causes, cheapest check first: (a) ERPNext held the `--small` August books (CC-304's tests posted them) when the fixtures were recorded, while the 2B was built from the full world; (b) the seeder skipped posting some invoices the 2B writer still emitted; (c) the key differs (`NormInvoiceNo`, bill date against posting date). Compare the world's August purchases, the ERPNext Purchase Invoices and the 2B file before choosing.
- **Not in this ticket:** `unrecorded_bank_charge` for `BNK-20260904-001` in sharma 2026-09 (a bank-rec finding); the re-record, the `suite-v1` fixtures and `evals/baseline.json` (a follow-up; fixtures and baseline go in together, the baseline in its own `baseline-update` commit).
- Rule on the check: if the right fix belongs in `internal/checks`, **stop** and report it so the orchestrator can split a standard implementer ticket. You never edit `internal/checks`.
- Protected paths: `evals/scenarios/**` is protected. Change `evals/scenarios/suite-v1/ground_truth/**` only if the orchestrator has told you the owner approved the change; otherwise propose the change in the diagnosis file.
