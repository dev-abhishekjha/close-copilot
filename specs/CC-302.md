# CC-302 · True-world event generator

## Goal

A pure function that turns a profile, a month and a seed into every business event that really happened that month.

## Context

- Ticket: `docs/implementation-tickets.md`, section `CC-302`. Shared specs: "Money, dates and IDs" (the `ext_id` format `EVT-sharma-2026-09-0412`) and "Company profile".
- Profiles and validation are in `internal/seed/profile.go` (CC-301). Read it: `Range`, `Day` (1–28 or `last`), `Share`/`Percent` (basis points), `Supplier.IsRegistered`, `IsAnnual`, `StartMonth`/`EndMonth`, `Profile.InState`, `SupplierGSTIN`.
- Money is `money.Paise` (`internal/money`, CC-203). Profile `_inr` fields are whole rupees: multiply by 100.
- **No floats.** CC-002's `nofloat` analyzer covers `internal/seed`, so never call `rand.Float64`, and do percentages in integer paise and basis points. Rounding is half away from zero, to the paisa.
- **No I/O in `Generate`.** No clock and no global rand: randomness comes only from `rand.New(rand.NewPCG(uint64(profile.Seed), hash(company, month)))` in `math/rand/v2`. `hash` is FNV-1a 64 over `company + "|" + month`. Never range over a map when generating; sort first.
- Downstream: the book writer (CC-304) turns events into ERPNext documents. The bank CSV (CC-305) uses `BankRef` and the bank-side events. The planter (CC-306) changes events. Keep the struct simple and JSON-friendly.
- No new dependencies.

## Subtasks (Phase 1: `suite-skeleton`, company `sharma`, any single month, scaled with `Small`)

- [ ] **`internal/seed/world.go`:**
  - `Event` struct with JSON tags (snake_case): `ExtID`, `Kind`, `Date` (`YYYY-MM-DD` string at the JSON edge, or a `time.Time` UTC midnight with custom marshalling; pick one), `Party`, `PartyType` (`Supplier`/`Customer`/empty), `Account`, `Taxable`, `IGST`, `CGST`, `SGST`, `Gross` (all `money.Paise`), `InvoiceNo`, `Refs` (ExtIDs this event settles, e.g. a payment's invoice), `BankRef`, `Narration`, `Meta` (`Description`, `ServiceFrom`, `ServiceTo`, plus `GSTRate`, `SupplierGSTIN` where relevant).
  - Kind constants: `sale`, `receipt`, `gateway_receipt`, `gateway_settlement`, `purchase`, `vendor_payment`, `payroll`, `bank_charge`, `interest`, `prepaid_amortisation`.
  - **Account name constants** with no company abbreviation, which CC-303 creates and CC-304 resolves as `<name> - <abbr>`:
    - bank: the profile's `bank.account`;
    - `Payment Gateway Clearing`, `Gateway Fees`, `Bank Charges`, `Rent`, `Electricity`, `Software Subscriptions`, `Prepaid Expenses`, `Repairs and Maintenance`, `Office Equipment`, `Salaries`, `Interest Income`;
    - `Sales`, `Debtors`, `Creditors`, which already exist in ERPNext;
    - one account per supplier kind for goods and services: `Cost of Goods Sold` and `Professional Charges` or similar. Name them, and list them all in one exported slice `Accounts` so CC-303 can create them.
    - Utilities other than power can map to a single `Telephone and Internet` account, or similar; just keep the list closed.
  - `World` struct: `Company`, `Month`, `OpeningBank` (Paise), `Events` sorted by `(Date, ExtID)`, and `ClosingBank`.
  - `Options{Small bool}`. `Small` scales invoice counts down (sales to about a tenth, at least 10), so CC-307's `--small` can use it.
  - `Generate(p Profile, month string, opt Options) (World, error)`: errors on a bad month. ExtIDs are `EVT-<company>-<YYYY-MM>-<4-digit seq>`, unique and assigned after sorting, so they're stable.
- [ ] **GST** (`generate_gst.go` or inside world.go):
  - Same state: CGST and SGST at half the rate each. Different state: IGST.
  - Round each invoice's tax to the paisa, half away from zero. CGST and SGST are each computed from the half rate, so an odd paisa can't make them differ.
  - Unregistered suppliers and rate 0 carry no tax.
  - `Gross = Taxable + taxes`.
- [ ] **Generators**, one file per family (`generate_sales.go`, `generate_purchases.go`, `generate_bank.go`, …):
  - **Sales and receipts:**
    - invoice count from `invoices_per_month`, customers `CUST-001…`, amount from `amount_inr`, rate from `gst_rates`;
    - customers are in-state unless you choose a mix; document it;
    - a share `gateway_share` (basis points) is collected through the gateway as `gateway_receipt` into Payment Gateway Clearing, the rest as direct `receipt` to the bank;
    - receipts fall 0–15 days after the sale, and only those inside the month are emitted.
  - **Gateway settlements:** weekly, net of `gateway_fee_pct` plus 18% GST on the fee, into the bank, with a `BankRef`. This is bank-side only: the books never record it (CC-304 posts nothing for it).
  - **Purchases**, per supplier pattern:
    - rent and utilities on their `day`;
    - goods and services without a `day` on generated days;
    - supplier invoice numbers like `CLD/2026/0912`, using a prefix from the supplier id, unique per supplier;
    - the annual subscription in its `renew_month`, booked to Prepaid Expenses, with `Meta` carrying the 12-month service period;
    - suppliers outside `start_month`/`end_month` are skipped.
  - **Prepaid amortisation:** `prepaid_amortisation` moves one twelfth of the annual subscription from Prepaid Expenses to Software Subscriptions:
    - in the renewal month and each later month of its service period;
    - Phase 1 computes it from the profile alone: the most recent renewal on or before the month;
    - the rounding remainder goes in the last month.
  - **One asset purchase per quarter:** a laptop, in the quarter's first month, from a goods supplier, posted to Office Equipment, above the capitalisation threshold (₹50,000).
  - **Vendor payments:** 5–20 days after the bill, with `Refs` set to the purchase, `BankRef` set, and only those inside the month emitted.
  - **Payroll:** on the last working day (Mon–Fri) of the month. `Day: last` means that day; a number means that date, or the previous working day.
  - **Bank charges:** monthly.
  - **Interest:** quarterly, in the last month of each quarter.
- [ ] **Bank balance:**
  - Start each month from `opening_bank_balance_inr` (Phase 1 has no chaining across months).
  - If a payment would take the balance below zero, defer it past the month end rather than emit it, so the invariant holds by construction.
- [ ] **`cmd/seed/main.go`:**
  - Add a `world` subcommand: `seed world --company sharma --month 2026-09 [--small] [--config config/companies]`.
  - It loads the profile, calls `Generate`, and prints indented JSON with a trailing newline to stdout.
  - `world` needs no ERPNext variables. Make the required env list depend on the subcommand, so `world` runs with an empty environment. Other subcommands keep today's behaviour ("not implemented yet").
  - `cli.Main`/`cli.Run` already parse `--version`; subcommand flags come after the subcommand name.
- [ ] **Tests:**
  - `TestWorldGolden`: `Generate(sharma, "2026-09", Options{})` marshalled exactly as `cmd/seed world` prints it equals `testdata/world-sharma-2026-09.json`. Add an `-update` flag that regenerates the file.
  - `TestWorldDeterministic`: two calls give deeply equal worlds, and a different month gives a different world.
  - `TestWorldInvariants`, for several months (2026-04 … 2026-12) and with `Small` on and off:
    - unique ExtIDs;
    - the running bank balance never goes negative;
    - each vendor payment is ≤ its invoice's gross, and its `Refs` point at a real purchase in the same world (or at an earlier month's bill, if you emit those; then document it);
    - `Gross == Taxable + IGST + CGST + SGST` for every taxed event;
    - IGST and CGST/SGST are never both set;
    - every `Account` is in `Accounts` or is the bank;
    - every event date is inside the month.
  - `TestGST`, table-driven: in-state vs out-of-state, a 5% and an 18% amount with an odd paisa, unregistered.
  - Coverage checks:
    - September (Cloudly's renewal month) has a prepaid purchase and an amortisation;
    - a quarter's first month has a laptop over ₹50,000;
    - the month has at least one event of every kind except `interest`, which appears in quarter-end months.
  - `cmd/seed/main_test.go`: `world` runs through `cli.Run` with no ERP env and prints JSON that decodes into `World`.

## Acceptance (human-readable)

- `go run ./cmd/seed world --company sharma --month 2026-09` prints the world as JSON. Running it twice gives identical bytes, and they equal the committed golden file.
- The invariant, determinism and GST tests pass. Nothing touches ERPNext, the network or the clock.

## Out of scope

- Writing anything to ERPNext (CC-303, CC-304), bank CSV and GSTR-2B (CC-305), planting errors (CC-306), and the other subcommands and suite files (CC-307).

## Notes

- `cmd/seed/main.go` isn't in the graph's files for this ticket. The ticket's acceptance needs the `world` subcommand, so this spec adds `main.go` and `main_test.go`. CC-307 owns the rest of the CLI.
- The golden file is large but deterministic. With `Small: false`, a month is a few hundred events, which is fine to commit.
