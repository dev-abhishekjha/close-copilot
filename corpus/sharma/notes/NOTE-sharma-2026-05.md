## Bank and gateway

HDFC Bank debits its current account charges every month, usually ₹1,000. Book them in the month they are debited (POL-001 section 7.1).

About 40% of sales are collected through the payment gateway, which keeps a fee of about 2% plus 18% GST on the fee. Book each settlement with its fee and GST (POL-001 section 7.2). Match bank lines to the ledger within 3 days.

## Payroll
