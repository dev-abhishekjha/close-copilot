## 2. Service

Licence and support for a software or cloud service used by the company, including updates and standard support.

## 3. Billing and amount

The supplier bills **annually, upfront**, once a year in September, for the twelve months that follow. The annual fee is ₹1,20,000 before GST.

Because the fee covers twelve months of service, the company books it as a prepaid expense and spreads it monthly over the service period.

## 4. GST

GST is charged at 18% as IGST, because the supplier is in another state (state code 27).

## 5. Service period

The agreement is effective from 1 April 2025 and renews each year in September, until ended by either party with 60 days' written notice.

## 6. Payment terms

The company pays each undisputed invoice within 45 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer. A disputed amount is raised in writing within seven days of receipt, and the undisputed part is paid on time.

## 7. Invoices

Each invoice quotes this contract reference, the service period it covers, the amount before GST and the tax. The supplier files its GST return in time so that the invoice appears in the company's GSTR-2B. A duplicate invoice for the same period is not payable.
