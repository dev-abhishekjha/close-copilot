## 2. Service

Supply of a utility service (power, connectivity or telecom) to the company's premises, charged on use for the billing month.

## 3. Billing and amount

The supplier bills **monthly**. The amount is variable, between ₹3,500 and ₹4,500 depending on use or supply a month before GST.

Invoices are raised on the 5th of the month.

## 4. GST

GST is charged at 18%, split into CGST at 9% and SGST at 9%, because the supplier is in the same state as the company.

## 5. Service period

The agreement is effective from 1 April 2025 and continues until ended by either party with 60 days' written notice.

## 6. Payment terms

The company pays each undisputed invoice within 15 days of the invoice date, by bank transfer. A disputed amount is raised in writing within seven days of receipt, and the undisputed part is paid on time.

## 7. Invoices

Each invoice quotes this contract reference, the service period it covers, the amount before GST and the tax. The supplier files its GST return in time so that the invoice appears in the company's GSTR-2B. A duplicate invoice for the same period is not payable.
